OUR MEETINGS — MVP DEMO

This prototype uses entirely dummy content in browser memory. Creating or editing meetings, agenda items, minutes, action items, status, and reminder states is for demonstration; refreshing the browser restores the sample data.

Included pages: Dashboard, Meeting Schedule, Meeting Details & Agenda/Minutes, Open Action Items, Meeting Minutes Archive, and Reminders.

Reminder activity is simulated. No email or WhatsApp notification is sent. There is no connected database or authentication in this demo build. The project can later be extended with persistent storage, committee roles, and real notification integrations.
